# Rumiku Brand & Landing Page

Rumiku is a creative digital ecosystem connecting gaming culture, fandom media, and community-driven platforms. This context covers the brand identity system and landing page component vocabulary.

## Language

### Brand Assets

**Logogram**:
The Kitsune mask icon used as a standalone brand mark. Silhouette inspired by Japanese pop culture and kitsune folklore.
_Avoid_: Logo, icon, mascot

**Wordmark**:
The custom "rumiku" text rendered in the brand's proprietary typeface. Available in italic/3D-skewed and straight/upright variants.
_Avoid_: Text logo, brand name, logotype

**Combo Mark**:
A horizontal lockup combining the wordmark and logogram side-by-side. Used in the navigation bar.
_Avoid_: Full logo, horizontal logo

**Stacked Logo**:
A vertical lockup with the wordmark below the logogram. Used in the footer and large-format placements.
_Avoid_: Vertical logo, block logo

### Color Tokens

**Electric Purple**:
The primary brand color (`#7d39ff`). Used for hero backgrounds, brand anchors, and primary accents.
_Avoid_: Brand purple, main purple

**Neon Lime**:
The high-visibility accent color (`#e7ff57`). Used for CTA buttons, badges, focus states, and the marquee text.
_Avoid_: Green, yellow, accent color

**Void Black**:
The darkest surface color (`#000000` / `#0A0A0C`). Used for borders, hard drop shadows, dark sections, and primary dark typography.
_Avoid_: Black, dark

### Visual Patterns

**Tactile Button**:
A button styled with a rounded-full border (`border-black/15` or transparent) and a glowing shadow (`shadow-glow`) that expands and shifts on `:hover` (`scale-105 shadow-glow-secondary`) to simulate a physical and digital press.
_Avoid_: Flat button, boring button

**Glowing Shadows & Glows**:
A solid or blurred colored glow applied to elements or backgrounds to create a playful, neon, and digital effect. Often paired with Mesh Gradients and Ascii backgrounds.
_Avoid_: Soft gray drop shadows, Neobrutalism

**Bento Grid**:
A CSS Grid layout with mixed cell sizes representing the ecosystem pillars. Not a uniform card grid.
_Avoid_: Card grid, feature grid

### Ecosystem Pillars

**Creedigo**:
The gaming and digital goods vertical. All-in-one game voucher top-up platform and digital goods distribution.
_Avoid_: Gaming platform, top-up service

**Glocult**:
The fandom media portal. Digital publication and culture hub covering anime, gaming, and pop culture.
_Avoid_: Blog, media site, news portal

**Rokupi**:
The merchandise and collectibles vertical. Exclusive fan-merchandise and official brand apparel.
_Avoid_: Merch store, shop
